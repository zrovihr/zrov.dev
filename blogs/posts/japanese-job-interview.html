There are some few patterns that I've learned from having interviews with Japanese companies.
They seem to dig you down on your project, once they finished, they will end the interview
with on of these two things,

1. Things that are completely unrelated at all, Like "do you like to find a good model for your games?"
2. Things that make you feel worthless to enter the company, Like "But, you didn't have a team experience right?"
or, "You have never made 「insert game genre / technique here」 type of game right?"

The first one wants to throw you off the topic and think of something else before closing.
The second one wants to politely turn you down. Honestly, it's funny seeing them trying to turn me down but I've always
have tricks up my sleeve. Nope! I have this, I have that. And they will spend minutes trying to find something that
I didn't do, but nobody else does anyway.
